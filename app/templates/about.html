About Finbyte

Democratizing credit access for MSMEs using innovation, data, and a commitment to financial inclusion.

Our Mission

In many rural and semi-urban areas, Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) are the backbone of the economy, yet they are often overlooked by traditional financial institutions due to a lack of formal credit history. **Finbyte exists to bridge this gap.** We utilize alternative data sources—like utility payments, mobile top-ups, and digital transaction patterns—to create an accurate, fair, and inclusive credit score where conventional models fail. Our goal is to empower these small businesses to grow, create jobs, and stabilize their communities.

The Finbyte Advantage

Alternative Data

We analyze non-traditional datasets to provide a score, ensuring MSMEs without bank records can still access capital.

High Accuracy Model

Our machine learning model is trained on diverse regional data, providing credit risk predictions with high confidence.

Financial Inclusion

Every score we generate pushes the boundary of who can participate in the formal economy.
